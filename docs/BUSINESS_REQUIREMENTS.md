# Business Requirements

## Vision

An AI-assisted website agency that automates discovery, research, demo creation, outreach, sales, delivery and maintenance, as far as is safe and lawful, with a human owner approving every external or financial action.

## Revenue streams

Website development; redesign; domain and hosting; maintenance subscriptions; SEO and digital presence; content and image services; automation integrations; custom features; monthly support.
Prices are never hardcoded: service packages and pricing are admin-configurable.

## Target industries

Restaurants, hotels, hospitals, clinics, manufacturers, retail, supermarkets, education, real estate, automobile, garages, professional services, others.

## Pilot

Belagavi, Karnataka, India. Multi-city, multi-state, multi-country by configuration.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Never invent business facts. Every fact carries its source, date, confidence and verification status (verified, unverified, AI suggestion, missing, customer-confirmed).
- No image reuse without confirmed rights.
- No scraping of restricted services; respect provider terms and robots.txt.
- No unapproved marketing messages; WhatsApp only via the official Cloud API with opt-in.
- No order marked paid without a verified gateway webhook.
- No paid service activated without owner approval.

## Key metrics

Project revenue, expenses, customer acquisition cost, gross margin, MRR, outstanding payments, cost per researched business, per demo, per qualified lead, per customer.
